What it does它能做什么

PolishPop lives in the menu bar. It reads only the text you deliberately select, rewrites it, and shows you the result before touching anything. PolishPop 常驻菜单栏。它只读取你主动选中的文字,润色之后先给你看结果,再决定要不要替换。

±

See every change改动一目了然

A word-level diff highlights what was added and removed — character by character for Chinese, which has no spaces between words. 逐词高亮增删内容;中文没有词间空格,所以按字高亮。

✎

Six use cases, five tones六种用途,五种语气

Email, chat message, social post, business update, announcement, or let it infer the format. Professional, natural, friendly, concise or executive. 邮件、即时消息、朋友圈、工作汇报、通知公告,或让它自动识别格式。语气可选专业、自然、亲切、精简、决断。

⏎

Replaces in place原地替换

The draft goes back into the field you selected it from. If the app will not accept a direct write, PolishPop pastes it and restores your previous clipboard. 润色结果写回你选中它的那个输入框。若该应用不接受直接写入,则临时用粘贴方式替换,随后恢复你原本的剪贴板。

↩

Instant mode, with undo快速模式,可撤销

An optional second shortcut skips the review panel entirely. Your original wording is kept so one click puts it back. 可选的第二个快捷键直接跳过校对面板。原文会被保留,一键即可还原。

◔

Never interrupts you不打断你

No modal dialogs and no focus stealing. Status appears as a small panel beside your cursor that goes away on its own. 没有模态对话框,不抢焦点。状态以光标旁的小提示出现,自动消失。

Free, and how sign-in works免费,以及登录方式

PolishPop costs nothing and sells nothing. It signs in to your own ChatGPT account and spends the Codex allowance that account already has. PolishPop 不收费,也不卖任何东西。它登录你自己的 ChatGPT 账号,消耗该账号本来就有的 Codex 额度。

  • The app这个 app Free. No licence key, no trial, no in-app purchase, no ads, no account with us — there is no "us" to have an account with. 完全免费。没有授权码、没有试用期、没有内购、没有广告,也不需要在我们这边注册账号——本来就没有「我们这边」。
  • Signing in登录方式 Codex's official OAuth flow, opened in your browser. You authorise on OpenAI's own page; PolishPop never sees your password. The token is issued to, stored by and refreshed by Codex in the macOS keychain — PolishPop does not read it. 使用 Codex 官方 OAuth 流程,在你的浏览器中完成。授权页面是 OpenAI 自己的,PolishPop 接触不到你的密码。令牌由 Codex 签发、保存在 macOS 钥匙串并自行刷新,PolishPop 不会读取它。
  • No API key不用 API Key There is nothing to paste and no API bill to pay. Rewrites draw on the Codex allowance included with an eligible ChatGPT plan. 没有任何密钥需要粘贴,也不会产生 API 账单。润色消耗的是符合条件的 ChatGPT 套餐所含的 Codex 额度。
  • What it cannot reach访问不到什么 Custom GPTs, your ChatGPT conversations, history and memory are all out of scope. PolishPop uses an isolated Codex home of its own. 自定义 GPT、你的 ChatGPT 对话、历史与记忆,一律不在范围内。PolishPop 使用自己独立的 Codex 配置目录。

You do need a ChatGPT plan that includes Codex access — that part is OpenAI's subscription, not ours. If your plan has it, PolishPop adds no cost on top. 前提是你的 ChatGPT 套餐包含 Codex 权限——那是 OpenAI 的订阅,不是我们的。只要套餐里有,PolishPop 不会再增加任何费用。

Installing安装步骤

  1. Unzip and move to Applications解压并移到「应用程序」

    Keep only one copy. Two copies of the same app confuse macOS about which one holds your Accessibility permission. 只保留一份。同一个 app 存在两份副本会让 macOS 分不清权限该给谁。

  2. Control-click the app, then choose Open按住 Control 点击 app,选择「打开」

    Only needed the first time. Double-clicking will be refused, because this build is not notarized by Apple. 只需第一次这样做。直接双击会被拒绝,因为这个版本没有经过 Apple 公证。

  3. Grant Accessibility permission授予辅助功能权限

    System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, then add PolishPop. It detects the change without a restart. 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 辅助功能,把 PolishPop 加进去。无需重启,它会自动检测到。

  4. Sign in with ChatGPT (Codex OAuth)使用 ChatGPT 登录(Codex OAuth)

    Open PolishPop's settings and choose Sign in with ChatGPT. Codex's official OAuth page opens in your browser; PolishPop never sees your password or your token. Nothing to pay, nothing to paste. 打开 PolishPop 设置,点「使用 ChatGPT 登录」。浏览器会打开 Codex 官方 OAuth 授权页,PolishPop 不会接触你的密码或令牌。不用付费,也不用粘贴任何密钥。

This build is signed, but not notarized.这个版本已签名,但未经 Apple 公证。

It is signed with a self-issued certificate so that your Accessibility permission survives updates, but Apple has not notarized it, so Gatekeeper will call the developer unidentified on first launch. Control-click → Open once and macOS remembers. 它使用自签发证书签名,好让辅助功能授权在更新后依然有效;但 Apple 没有对它公证,所以首次启动时 Gatekeeper 会提示开发者身份不明。按住 Control 点击 → 打开,一次之后 macOS 就会记住。

Privacy隐私

Text leaves your Mac only when you ask for a rewrite — never while you are simply selecting or typing. 只有你主动要求润色时,文字才会离开你的电脑——只是选中或打字时不会。

  • What is sent发送什么 Only the selection, and only after you click the sparkle button or press the shortcut. 仅发送你选中的那段文字,且仅在你点击闪光按钮或按下快捷键之后。
  • Where it goes发送到哪 To OpenAI through the official Codex app server, in a fresh throwaway session each time, with transcript history disabled. 经由官方 Codex 服务发送给 OpenAI,每次都是全新的一次性会话,且已关闭记录留存。
  • What is stored保存什么 Nothing. PolishPop keeps the last result in memory for five minutes so you can copy it again, then drops it. 不保存。最近一次结果仅在内存中保留五分钟供你再次复制,之后即丢弃。
  • What is refused拒绝读取 Password fields and DRM-protected content are never read. 密码输入框和受 DRM 保护的内容,一律不读取。

Your ChatGPT plan's own data controls, retention rules and usage limits still apply. Do not select passwords, banking details, private keys or confidential records. 你的 ChatGPT 套餐自身的数据控制、留存规则与用量限制依然适用。请勿选中密码、银行信息、私钥或机密资料。
